Purpose

This Application Security and Confidentiality Statement describes the application-level security, confidentiality, and data-handling considerations for XAIDA. It is intended to support informed use of the platform by auditors, organizations, and deployment administrators.

This statement is not a security certification, compliance attestation, legal opinion, warranty, or guarantee that a specific deployment is secure. The overall security of a XAIDA deployment depends on the configuration, operation, and governance of the hosting environment and the organization using the application.


## Confidentiality and Data Handling

XAIDA is designed to process audit-related information that may contain confidential, proprietary, regulated, or otherwise sensitive organizational data.

Evaluation metrics are stored separately from audit evidence. Local runs use
SQLite; the Cloud Run configuration stores them in Cloud SQL PostgreSQL.

When Cloud Run persistence is enabled, compressed snapshots of the active
audit session—including uploaded data, findings, audit log, and Vertex AI
assistant conversation—are stored in the private Cloud Storage bucket and
owner-bound
to the signed-in auditor. Snapshots expire after 24 hours without an autosave.
Exit, Reset, and New Analysis delete the active snapshot and its stored
artifacts; evaluation records remain under their separate retention policy.
Local runs keep the audit session in application memory.

When the Vertex AI assistant is enabled, relevant conversation content and
audit context may also be sent to the organization's approved Vertex AI model.

All information uploaded to, generated by, or processed within XAIDA should be handled in accordance with the organization's information-classification, privacy, confidentiality, retention, and data-protection requirements.

Users are responsible for ensuring that:

Only authorized information is uploaded and processed.
Access to audit evidence, analyses, reports, and exports is restricted to authorized personnel.
Confidential information is shared only through approved organizational channels.
Applicable contractual, regulatory, legal, privacy, and retention requirements are observed.
Data uploaded to XAIDA is appropriate for processing within the organization's approved technology environment.

XAIDA does not alter the ownership, classification, confidentiality, or regulatory status of any information processed through the application. Responsibility for determining whether information may be uploaded, processed, exported, stored, or transmitted remains with the organization and its authorized users.

## Authorization Before Use

Before processing audit data within XAIDA, organizations should:

Restrict access to authorized users.
Configure and validate OIDC authentication and email allowlisting.
Review the hosting environment, network controls, backup strategy, monitoring, logging, and retention requirements.
Approve any configured AI services and associated data-processing arrangements.
Verify that uploaded information is authorized for use within the deployment.
Establish appropriate policies governing access, storage, export, and deletion of audit-related information.

## Access Control and Session Security

XAIDA supports OIDC authentication and email allowlisting to help restrict access to authorized users.

Security of authentication, identity lifecycle management, account provisioning, multi-factor authentication, password policies, access reviews, and user deprovisioning remains the responsibility of the deployment owner and identity provider.

Organizations should implement the principle of least privilege and periodically review:

User access rights
Service accounts
Administrative privileges
Authentication configurations
Access and activity logs

Audit information is maintained within the active application session to
support workflow activities. Cloud Run deployments can autosave and resume
user-isolated sessions using the configured Cloud SQL and Cloud Storage
backends.

Session snapshots are not a permanent audit archive. Cloud Storage lifecycle
deletion is asynchronous; use the configured cleanup job and verify deletion
against organizational requirements. Ending or clearing a session should not
be interpreted as cryptographic erasure. Organizations should implement and
maintain approved mechanisms for access, backup, retention, recovery, and
deletion of audit information.

## Security Controls and Deployment Responsibilities

XAIDA provides application-level safeguards but relies on the underlying deployment environment for infrastructure security.

Deployment owners are responsible for implementing and maintaining appropriate security controls, including:

Network segmentation and firewall protections.
Encryption in transit.
Encryption at rest where applicable.
Secure secrets and credential management.
Vulnerability management and software patching.
Endpoint and server security controls.
Backup and disaster-recovery arrangements.
Monitoring, logging, and alerting.
Incident response procedures.
Administrative access management.
Periodic security reviews and risk assessments.

The effectiveness of these controls depends upon proper implementation and ongoing operational governance by the organization.

## Artificial Intelligence and External Processing

XAIDA may incorporate AI-assisted functionality to support analysis, explanation, narrative generation, or user interactions.

When enabled, relevant information from the active audit session may be transmitted to the organization's approved Vertex AI model to generate responses or analytical outputs.

Prior to enabling AI-assisted functionality, organizations should evaluate whether audit evidence, personal data, confidential information, regulated information, or proprietary information may be transmitted to external services and determine whether such use complies with applicable policies and requirements.

Organizations are responsible for:

Approving the selected AI service.
Reviewing applicable data-handling terms.
Assessing privacy and confidentiality implications.
Determining whether AI-assisted processing is appropriate for the information involved.

AI-generated content may be incomplete, inaccurate, misleading, or based on incorrect assumptions. AI-generated outputs should not be treated as audit findings, conclusions, or evidence without appropriate human review.

## Evaluation Metadata

XAIDA may store evaluation and performance metrics separately from active audit-session data for operational and system-evaluation purposes.

These records are subject to the configured retention period and are distinct from audit evidence maintained within user workflows.

The evaluation data store is not intended to serve as a permanent audit repository or records-management system.

## Exports and Generated Reports

XAIDA can generate and export audit outputs, including reports, spreadsheets, and other supporting documentation.

Exported files may contain confidential information, business-sensitive information, audit evidence, personally identifiable information, or regulated data.

Once exported, these files exist outside XAIDA's application controls and should be protected using the organization's approved:

Access-control mechanisms
Storage locations
Encryption requirements
Retention schedules
Data-loss-prevention measures
Secure transfer procedures

Spreadsheet exports are configured to preserve formula-like text as text where applicable; however, users should review exported files before opening, distributing, or sharing them.

## Human Oversight and Professional Judgment

XAIDA is an analytical support tool intended to assist audit activities. It does not replace professional judgment, auditor expertise, management review, or established audit methodologies.

Anomaly indicators, risk assessments, narratives, explanations, recommendations, and AI-generated outputs should be regarded as supporting information until independently reviewed and validated.

Users remain responsible for:

Assessing the reliability of evidence.
Evaluating analytical results.
Documenting conclusions.
Exercising professional judgment.
Reviewing generated outputs.
Approving final reports and audit decisions.

## Limitations and Disclaimer

XAIDA is provided as an analytical and decision-support platform.

No representation is made that XAIDA alone satisfies the requirements of any security, privacy, audit, regulatory, governance, risk-management, or compliance framework.

Security, privacy, confidentiality, and compliance outcomes depend upon:

Organizational policies and procedures.
Deployment architecture and configuration.
Infrastructure security controls.
User access management.
Operational governance.
Effective human oversight.

Organizations should conduct their own assessments to determine whether XAIDA is appropriate for their environment, risk profile, regulatory obligations, and audit objectives.
